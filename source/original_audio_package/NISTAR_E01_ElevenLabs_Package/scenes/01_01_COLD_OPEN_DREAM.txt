SCRIPTURE: [low and solemn] Pride walks before ruin. Arrogance before collapse.

REN_VO: [quietly] There are men who need rules.

REN_VO: Men who need warning signs.

REN_VO: Men who need brakes.

REN_VO: [with cold certainty] I was never one of them.

REN: [under his breath] I always make it.

REN_VO: The world moves for men who refuse to stop.

THE_VOICE: [deep, calm, immense] Who told you that you were driving?
