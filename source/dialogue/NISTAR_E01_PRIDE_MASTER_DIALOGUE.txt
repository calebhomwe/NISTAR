===== SCENE 01: 01 COLD OPEN DREAM =====

SCRIPTURE: [low and solemn] Pride walks before ruin. Arrogance before collapse.

REN_VO: [quietly] There are men who need rules.

REN_VO: Men who need warning signs.

REN_VO: Men who need brakes.

REN_VO: [with cold certainty] I was never one of them.

REN: [under his breath] I always make it.

REN_VO: The world moves for men who refuse to stop.

THE_VOICE: [deep, calm, immense] Who told you that you were driving?



===== SCENE 02: 02 PRISON PRAYER AND AWAKENING =====

DAISUKE: [gasps] Jesus—

DAISUKE: [breathing shakily] I don't know where my son is.

DAISUKE: But You do.

DAISUKE: [quietly, with urgency] Don't let the lie he calls strength kill him.

REN: [gasps sharply]

REN: [hoarse] Yeah.

SATO: [coldly] You are late.

REN: I'm at home.

SATO: Not anymore.

REN: What happened?

SATO: Arrive first. Ask questions second.

REN: Sato—

SATO: And Ren?

SATO: [after a short pause] You were already late before the phone rang.



===== SCENE 03: 03 EMPTY TANK =====

REN: [quietly] At least you don't complain.

REN: [under his breath] I always make it.

REN_VO: Sato only calls when money is moving.

REN_VO: Money moves when someone is afraid.

REN_VO: Tonight, somebody is afraid.

REN_VO: [dismissive] I'll eat afterwards.



===== SCENE 04: 04 REINA SENSES THE PRAYER =====

ASSOCIATE: Was that him?

REINA: [calmly] No.

REINA: Something reached for him.

ASSOCIATE: Your people?

REINA: [a faint amused breath] My people do not pray.

REINA: [quietly] Find out who did.



===== SCENE 05: 05 THE INTERVENTION =====

REN: [low, impatient] Move.

REN: [shouts] No—

REN: [stunned] What—

GUARDIAN: [exhausted, almost a whisper] Again.

REN: [breathes shakily, then laughs once] Still got it.

DAISUKE: [softly] Thank You.

REFLECTION: [whispers] Run.

REN: [adrenaline rising] You want a show?



===== SCENE 06: 06 THE CHASE =====

REN: [laughing under his breath] You're all too slow.

REN: [shouts over the engine] They'll never catch me!

REN: [angrily] Not tonight.



===== SCENE 07: 07 SATO AND TANK =====

SATO: You are late.

REN: I'm here.

SATO: That was not the accusation.

REN: [confused] What is that?

SATO: Tank.

REN: Tank?

SATO: He got out.

REN: You called me across the city for a dog?

SATO: I called you because he was missing.

REN: I had police behind me.

SATO: Yes.

SATO: You crossed half the city like God Himself was chasing you.

SATO: [after a beat] For a dog.

REN: You said it was urgent.

SATO: It was urgent to me.

SATO: Your problem is not lateness.

SATO: Your problem is that you think urgency makes you important.

SATO: [quietly] Pride makes noise.

SATO: Power does not have to.

SATO: Next time you bring police to my door, I take a finger.

SATO: Not because I need it.

SATO: Because you do.

SATO: Now leave.

SATO: [lower and colder] Quietly.



===== SCENE 08: 08 THE LIE AND THE WALLET =====

REN_VO: Machines are wrong all the time.

REN: [frustrated] Come on.

REN_VO: Machines give up.

REN_VO: People give up.

REN_VO: [with absolute certainty] I don't.

MENTOR: This yours?

REN: Where did you get that?

MENTOR: You dropped it.

REN: Take the money.

MENTOR: There isn't any.

REN: Then why follow me?

MENTOR: You should be dead.

REN: [dryly] I get that a lot.

MENTOR: No.

MENTOR: You don't.

REN: Who are you?

MENTOR: Someone who was told to stop.

REN: By who?

MENTOR: [quietly] You already know.

REN: You walked through rain for a wallet and a sermon?

MENTOR: No.

MENTOR: I walked through rain because tonight was the closest you have ever come to understanding that you are not immortal.



===== SCENE 09: 09 THE PRAYER AND THE CALL =====

REN: [sharply] Don't touch me.

MENTOR: Then listen.

MENTOR: [calm, direct prayer] Jesus Christ—

MENTOR: Open his eyes before the darkness teaches him to call mercy skill.

REINA: [through the phone, intimate and controlled] Come home, Ren.

REINA: Before your father's God takes anything else from me.

REN: [stunned, barely audible] My father is dead.



===== SCENE 10: 10 POST CREDIT =====

GUARD: Kisaragi.

GUARD: You have a visitor.

DAISUKE: Who?

GUARD: [uneasy] Your wife.
