SATO: You are late.

REN: I'm here.

SATO: That was not the accusation.

REN: [confused] What is that?

SATO: Tank.

REN: Tank?

SATO: He got out.

REN: You called me across the city for a dog?

SATO: I called you because he was missing.

REN: I had police behind me.

SATO: Yes.

SATO: You crossed half the city like God Himself was chasing you.

SATO: [after a beat] For a dog.

REN: You said it was urgent.

SATO: It was urgent to me.

SATO: Your problem is not lateness.

SATO: Your problem is that you think urgency makes you important.

SATO: [quietly] Pride makes noise.

SATO: Power does not have to.

SATO: Next time you bring police to my door, I take a finger.

SATO: Not because I need it.

SATO: Because you do.

SATO: Now leave.

SATO: [lower and colder] Quietly.
